SessionNote

Your clients, your notes, your device.

The private session notebook for solo therapists, psychologists, physiotherapists, massage therapists, dietitians and coaches.

Notes in seconds

  • SOAP and DAP templates, plus free-form notes
  • On-device dictation and note drafts you review before saving
  • Session history, tags and a session timer per client

Hand it to your client

  • Consent forms with signature, saved with date and time
  • PHQ-9 and GAD-7 with automatic scoring
  • The rest of the app stays locked until you unlock it with Face ID or your passcode

Appointments and payment log

Today's appointments, a simple calendar, cash/card/transfer payment records and PDF/CSV export. SessionNote does not process payments.

Private by design

No server. No account. No analytics. Your records stay on your iPhone or iPad. Privacy policy

Pricing

Free with one client. Unlimited clients with a one-time lifetime purchase or an optional monthly plan.
