Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · Türkçe

SessionNote is a note-taking and record-keeping app for solo practitioners such as therapists, psychologists, physiotherapists, massage therapists, dietitians and coaches. It is designed so that the records you keep about your clients stay on your device. SessionNote has no server and no account system, and we (the developer) cannot access your data.

Client records and health information

Client details, session notes, dictation text, appointment and payment records, questionnaire answers (such as PHQ-9 and GAD-7), consent texts and signatures are stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad. This can include health information about your clients. We do not upload, read, analyse, sell or share any of it, we do not use it for advertising or data mining, and we do not use HealthKit.

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, your device backup can include SessionNote data. That backup is stored under Apple's terms in your own account. If you delete a client, their sessions, forms and payments are deleted from the app. If you delete the app, all of its data is deleted with it, except for files you exported yourself.

You are the data controller

You decide what you record about your clients and you are responsible for it under the law that applies to you (for example GDPR, KVKK or HIPAA). SessionNote is a tool on your device; because we never receive your clients' data, we do not process it on your behalf.

Dictation and note drafts

Dictation uses Apple's speech recognizer in on-device mode only; if your language has no on-device model, dictation is not offered. Note drafts are generated on your device with Apple's on-device language model. Audio is not stored. Your device may download Apple language models from Apple; these downloads do not include your data.

Face ID and passcode

App lock and client mode use Apple's LocalAuthentication. Face ID data never leaves the Secure Enclave and is never available to the app.

Exports

PDF and CSV files are created only when you tap Export, and are handed to the iOS share sheet. Where they go next is your choice.

Purchases

SessionNote Pro is sold as an in-app purchase processed by Apple. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple's StoreKit on the device.

What we do not collect

  • No client data, notes, forms or signatures
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No contacts, location or photos
  • No sale or sharing of personal data

Children

SessionNote is intended for professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
